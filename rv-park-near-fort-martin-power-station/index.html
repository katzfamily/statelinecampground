RV sites near Fort Martin Power Station.

A quiet, full-hookup base for outage and turnaround crews working the plant. Monthly rates, back-in sites up to 43 feet, free WiFi and laundry, open year-round.

If you are working a project at Fort Martin Power Station, State Line Campground is a practical place to park the RV. We are a family-owned park a short drive from the plant near Maidsville, WV — roughly 20 to 25 minutes — with full hookups on every site, free WiFi and laundry, and year-round operation for outages that run into the winter.

Bringing a crew? Call Dana and Rod at 304-376-5970 and we will set up several sites together and work out monthly rates for the length of the outage.

Built for plant work

  • Full 30 and 50 amp hookups on back-in sites that fit rigs up to 43 feet, with water, sewer, and waste disposal.
  • Monthly and short-term rates that flex to a two-week outage or a longer turnaround.
  • Free WiFi and free laundry, so you can wind down without leaving camp.
  • Quiet grounds for rest between long shifts, with owners five minutes away.
  • Open 365 days a year, including winter outages.
  • Crew sites together — call and we will group your rigs.

Close to the plant and to town

State Line sits on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border in Blacksville, WV, a short drive from Fort Martin and about 25 minutes from Morgantown. Gas, groceries, and restaurants are less than two miles away, so it is an easy in-and-out around your shift. Working elsewhere in the area too? See our monthly sites for work crews.

Getting here

State Line Campground is on 186 Cliff Rd in Blacksville, WV 26521. Call ahead and the owners will give you turn-by-turn directions from the plant, or open the campground in Google Maps.
