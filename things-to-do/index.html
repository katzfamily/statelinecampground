Things to do near State Line Campground.

Parks, fishing, swimming, and good local food, all a short drive from camp in Blacksville, WV. Dana and Rod know the area well and are happy to point you to their favorite spots.

Close to camp

State Line Campground keeps you near the things that make a trip easy. Gas, groceries, and restaurants are less than two miles away, so you do not have to plan every meal ahead — Creek N' Rail Cafe is a local favorite close to camp, and there is a Huntington Bank with a drive-thru ATM nearby — handy for grabbing cash or depositing a paycheck on a long work day. Mason-Dixon Historical Park, a 396-acre county park a short drive away, is a favorite for families, with three playgrounds, large pavilions, clean restrooms, a baseball field, hiking trails along the water, and even a fairy trail for the kids. There are also ponds and lakes across the region for fishing.

The local library is only three minutes away by car and is a favorite on a rainy afternoon. It runs story hours for children and lends out games and toys that families can check out and bring back to camp.

A short drive to Morgantown and Waynesburg

Both Morgantown, WV and Waynesburg, PA are roughly 25 minutes from the campground. Morgantown adds college-town restaurants, shopping, and WVU events, while Waynesburg and Greene County offer their own parks and small-town stops. When you get back to camp, the quiet returns.

Ask the owners. Dana and Rod have lived in this area for several generations and know the back roads, fishing holes, and the best coffee within thirty miles. Call 304-376-5970.
