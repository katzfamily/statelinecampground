RV camping near Morgantown, WV.

State Line Campground is a family-owned RV park about 25 minutes from Morgantown, West Virginia, with full hookups on every site and the quiet of the WV/PA border.

If you are looking for RV camping near Morgantown, WV, State Line Campground gives you full hookups and a quiet setting about 25 minutes from town. We are a small, family-owned campground in Blacksville, right on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border, run by owners Dana and Rod who live five minutes away.

Morgantown is an easy drive for a WVU game weekend, a work trip, or a visit to the hospital and university, while you stay somewhere calm at night. Every site has 30 and 50 amp hookups, and your rate covers electricity, water, sewage, waste disposal, and WiFi. Laundry and firewood are free.

We are open 365 days a year, so the campground works for a single night, a full week, or a monthly stay. Reservations are not required, though we appreciate a call so we can have a site ready for you. Coming from the Pennsylvania side instead? See campgrounds near Waynesburg, PA.

What every site includes

  • Full hookups with 30 and 50 amp electric service
  • Water, sewer, and waste disposal
  • Back-in sites that fit rigs up to 43 feet
  • Free WiFi at your site
  • Free laundry, with good washers and dryers
  • Free firewood delivered to your site
  • Clean bathhouse with showers and fire pits (picnic tables available on request)
  • Pet friendly, with a dog walking service available

Getting here from Morgantown

State Line Campground is on Cliff Rd in Blacksville, WV 26521, about a 25 minute drive from Morgantown. Call ahead and the owners will give you turn-by-turn directions from your route. You can also open the campground in Google Maps.
