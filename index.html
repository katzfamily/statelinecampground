About Stateline Campground

A campground run by people who care.

Stateline Campground sits right on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border in Blacksville, WV. We are a short drive from both Waynesburg, PA and Morgantown, WV, yet far enough out that the noise drops away. Owners Dana and Rod run the place themselves and live five minutes from the grounds.

Our family has lived in this area for several generations. We know every fishing pond, every back road, and every shortcut to good coffee within thirty miles.

Reservations are not required, but we appreciate them. Because we live so close, if you need something during your stay, we are there. We offer a dog walking service while you are at work or out on an adventure. And yes, the laundry and the firewood are free.

Common questions

Good to know.

Where is Stateline Campground located?
Stateline Campground is in Blacksville, West Virginia, right on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border. It is a short drive from Morgantown, WV and Waynesburg, PA, with gas, groceries, and restaurants less than two miles away.
Is the campground open year-round?
Yes. Stateline Campground is open 365 days a year, including winter. Owners Dana and Rod live five minutes away and are available when you need them.
What hookups and amenities are included?
Every site has full hookups with both 30 and 50 amp service. Your rate includes electricity, water, sewage, waste disposal, and WiFi. Laundry and firewood are free. The grounds have a clean bathhouse with showers, picnic tables, fire pits, and drinking water.
Do I need a reservation to stay?
Reservations are not required, but they are appreciated. Call ahead at 304-376-5970 to check availability.
Are there campgrounds near Waynesburg, PA with full hookups?
Yes. Stateline Campground is about 25 minutes from Waynesburg, PA, just across the state line in Blacksville, WV, and every site has full hookups with 30 and 50 amp electric service, water, and sewer. It is also open 365 days a year, including winter, which most campgrounds in the area are not. See our guide to campgrounds near Waynesburg, PA.
Is the campground pet friendly?
Yes, Stateline Campground is pet friendly. Aggressive animals are not permitted. The owners also offer a dog walking service while you are at work or out exploring.
