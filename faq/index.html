Frequently asked questions.

Hookups, rates, pets, reservations, and directions for Stateline Campground in Blacksville, West Virginia. Still have a question? Call Dana and Rod at 304-376-5970.

Where is Stateline Campground located?
Stateline Campground is in Blacksville, West Virginia, right on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border. It is a short drive from Morgantown, WV and Waynesburg, PA, with gas, groceries, and restaurants less than two miles away.
Is Stateline Campground open year-round?
Yes. Stateline Campground is open 365 days a year, including winter. Owners Dana and Rod live five minutes away and are available when you need them.
What hookups does each site have?
Every site has full hookups with both 30 and 50 amp electric service, plus water and sewer. Your rate also includes WiFi and waste disposal.
What is included in the rate?
Your rate covers electricity, water, sewage, waste disposal, and WiFi. Laundry and firewood are free. The grounds also have a clean bathhouse with showers, fire pits, and drinking water, with picnic tables available on request.
Do I need a reservation to stay?
Reservations are not required, but they are appreciated. Call ahead at 304-376-5970 to check availability.
How much does it cost to stay?
Stateline offers daily, weekly, and monthly rates, with discounts for longer stays. Call 304-376-5970 for current rates. The campground accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and Venmo.
Is laundry really free?
Yes. Laundry is free for guests, and the washers and dryers are good machines. You do not need quarters.
Is firewood provided?
Yes. Firewood is free and the owners deliver it right to your site.
Is the campground pet friendly?
Yes, Stateline Campground is pet friendly. Aggressive animals are not permitted. The owners also offer a dog walking service while you are at work or out exploring.
Can I stay long term or for a season?
Yes. Monthly sites are available and are a good fit for seasonal stays or longer projects in the area. Call to ask about current monthly availability.
How far is the campground from Morgantown, WV?
Stateline Campground is roughly a 25 minute drive from Morgantown, WV. It is a similar drive from Waynesburg, PA.
How far is the campground from Waynesburg, PA?
Stateline Campground is about a 25 minute drive from Waynesburg, PA, just across the state line in Blacksville, WV. It is the closest full-hookup campground to Waynesburg that stays open all year. More detail on the campgrounds near Waynesburg, PA page.
What payment methods do you accept?
Stateline accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and Venmo.
