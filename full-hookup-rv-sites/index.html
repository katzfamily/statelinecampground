Full hookup RV sites in West Virginia.

Every site at State Line Campground is a full hookup site — 30 and 50 amp electric, water, sewer, and waste disposal — on the quiet WV/PA border near Morgantown.

Looking for a campground where every site is a full hookup RV site? At State Line Campground, you never have to hunt for the right spot or ask whether a site has sewer. All of our sites come with 30 and 50 amp electric service, water, sewer, and on-site waste disposal — the complete "full hookup" every RVer wants. We are a small, family-owned campground on 186 Cliff Rd in Blacksville, WV, right on the West Virginia and Pennsylvania border, run by owners Dana and Rod who live five minutes from the grounds.

A true full hookup means all three connections are at your site: power, fresh water, and sewer. That is exactly what you get here — no shared dump station trips mid-stay, no partial hookups, no surprises. Your rate covers electricity, water, sewage, waste disposal, and WiFi, and both laundry and firewood are free for guests.

We are open 365 days a year, so full hookup sites are available for a single night, a full week, or a monthly stay — winter included. Reservations are not required, though we appreciate a call so we can have a site ready for you. Working a longer job in the area? See our monthly sites for work crews and traveling nurses.

30 and 50 amp on every site. Full hookups, back-in sites up to 43 feet, open year-round. Call Dana and Rod at 304-376-5970 for current rates and availability.

What every full hookup site includes

  • 30 and 50 amp electric service on every site
  • Fresh water hookup at your site
  • Sewer and on-site waste disposal
  • Back-in sites that fit rigs up to 43 feet
  • Free WiFi at your site
  • Free laundry, with good washers and dryers
  • Free firewood delivered to your site
  • Clean bathhouse with showers and fire pits (picnic tables available on request)
  • Pet friendly, with a dog walking service available

Full hookups for work, travel, or a family weekend

Because every site is a full hookup with 30 and 50 amp power, State Line Campground works just as well for a crew working a gas, energy, or pipeline job as it does for a family passing through or a traveling nurse on contract at the Morgantown hospitals. Big rig or small, you get the same complete hookups and the same quiet grounds.

Where we are

State Line Campground is on 186 Cliff Rd in Blacksville, WV 26521, about 25 minutes from Morgantown and a short drive from Fairmont, Mannington, and Waynesburg, PA. Call ahead and the owners will give you turn-by-turn directions from your route. You can also open the campground in Google Maps.

Full hookup site questions

Does every site have full hookups?
Yes. Every site is a full hookup site with 30 and 50 amp electric, water, sewer, and waste disposal. Free WiFi is included, and laundry and firewood are free.
Do you have 50 amp full hookup RV sites?
Yes. Every site offers both 30 and 50 amp service, so you do not have to request a specific amperage. Call 304-376-5970 to reserve.
What size RV fits at a full hookup site?
Our back-in full hookup sites fit rigs up to 43 feet. If you are towing or arriving after dark, call ahead and the owners will help you get parked.
Are full hookup sites available in winter?
Yes. We are open 365 days a year, including winter, so full hookup sites are available year-round for daily, weekly, and monthly stays.
